Common conditions

What patients come in with.

General information only — it is not a diagnosis. If something is painful or getting worse, book a consultation.

01

Knee pain & arthritis

Assessment of cartilage wear, meniscus injury and alignment, with a staged plan before any surgical option is discussed.

02

Lower back & sciatica

Nerve-pattern pain often settles with targeted physiotherapy. We identify when imaging or referral is genuinely needed.

03

Shoulder & rotator cuff

Frozen shoulder, impingement and cuff tears — treated with a graded loading programme in most cases.

04

Sports & gym injuries

Ligament sprains, tendinopathy and overuse injuries, with a structured return-to-training timeline.

05

Post-operative rehab

Structured recovery after joint replacement or ligament reconstruction, coordinated with your surgeon.
